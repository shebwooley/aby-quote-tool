About the administrator

ABY Benefits LLC

Founded 1986

ABY Benefits LLC is a Dallas–Fort Worth-based third-party administrator specializing in flexible benefit plans and compliance solutions. Headquartered in Plano, Texas, ABY helps employers nationwide reduce healthcare costs while offering strong, competitive benefit packages.

Services include HRA, FSA, HSA, DCAP, COBRA, HIPAA, ERISA wrap documents, and ACA Forms 1094/1095-C. ABY supports employers of all sizes and industries, including groups with unique needs or complex plan designs. ABY also provides continuing education courses across the U.S. on Sections 125 and 105, COBRA, ACA compliance, ethics, and related topics.
